Side Streams

Yasmin Adele Majeed’s debut novel, Side Streams, is forthcoming from Little, Brown.
“Powerful and surprising, carrying us through the secrets and ruptures of childhood and the rebuilding of a family in a new light. Across decades and continents, love and loss, this story is both intimate and sweeping. It is at once a queer coming-of-age story, an exploration of art and friendship across decades, and a poignant consideration of diaspora, return, and what it might take to start again.” — Eliana Ramage, author of To the Moon and Back
“Yasmin Adele Majeed weaves desire, art-making, and noticing itself into this Bay Area immigrant community in prose so tender I felt as though I’d known these characters for years.” — Nishanth Injam, author of The Best Possible Experience
“Only one book in and Majeed is already an essential voice.” — Anthony Marra, author of Mercury Pictures Presents
“A sweeping yet intimate debut novel that explores family secrets, the bonds of friendship, art’s power to impact communities, and more.” — Daphne Palasi Andreades, author of Lucid Dreams and Brown Girls
